## Brand & Style

This design system channels a refined coastal-tech aesthetic built specifically for property managers, boutique operators, and independent vacation rental hosts in Florianópolis. It reconciles the effortless vitality of the Santa Catarina coastline with the mission-critical rigor of a high-yield enterprise SaaS platform.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Coastal Precision:** Clean, sunlit, and uncluttered. It evokes ocean air, clarity, and ease while maintaining meticulous enterprise-grade structure.
- **Data-Driven Confidence:** Hosts entrust their livelihoods to this platform. The interface conveys stability, dynamic revenue optimization, and operational mastery without visual noise.
- **Approachability & Fluidity:** Optimized for on-the-move management—inspecting beach villas, coordinating turnovers between check-outs, and adjusting dynamic nightly rates directly from mobile devices.

### Design Movement
**Modern Coastal Tech (Contemporary SaaS with Ambient Tactility):** A disciplined hybrid of clean minimalism, soft ambient surface elevations, micro-glass navigation bars, and crisp typographic hierarchy. Surfaces leverage crisp whites, pale maritime tinted slates, and subtle oceanic depth rather than harsh flat lines.

## Colors

The palette captures Florianópolis's deep waters, lagoons, and sun-drenched architecture, anchored by functional enterprise UI fundamentals.

### Core Palette
- **Primary (`#0A2540` - Deep Ocean Navy):** The structural anchor used for primary text, primary button containers, critical navigation markers, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0077B6` - Atlantic Cerulean):** Expressive interactive color for links, selected tabs, progress bars, and high-priority action affordances.
- **Tertiary (`#00B4D8` - Coastal Lagoon Cyan):** Used for micro-indicators, chart curves, interactive sliders, and subtle focus glows. Pairs with a light wash (`#90E0EF`) for selected surface tints.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Neutral):** Provides balanced legibility for captions, secondary metadata, inactive states, and structural hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`).

### Semantic & Seasonal Accents
- **Seasonal Alert & Yield Multiplier (`#F59E0B` - Sunset Amber):** Reserved strictly for high-demand spikes, *Alta Temporada* (peak season) badges, premium pricing opportunities, and warning flags.
- **Positive Yields & Occupancy Health (`#10B981` - Emerald Growth):** Applied to positive RevPAR changes, revenue spikes, confirmed reservations, and instant cleaning completion statuses.
- **Surfaces:** Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, layered over a refreshing maritime mist canvas (`#F8FAFC`).

## Typography

The type system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a balance of geometric clarity, open apertures, and friendly contemporary curves suited for mobile rental operations.

### Hierarchy & Mobile Behavior
- **Numeric Optimization:** Financial figures (daily rates in BRL, occupancy ratios, payout splits) utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain strict column alignment across metric tables and performance sheets.
- **Mobile Scale Adjustments:** Hero revenue summaries and dashboard stats leverage `numeric-metric` for fast glanceability under bright coastal sunlight.
- **Labels & Badges:** `label-sm` applies uppercase transformation with tracking (`0.04em`) when designating season states (*ALTA TEMPORADA*, *BAIXA TEMPORADA*) and cleaning stages (*EM LIMPEZA*, *PRONTO*).

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first, strict 4px/8px incremental spacing system designed around fluid card modules and high thumb-zone reachability.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Structure:** Fluid 4-column layout on mobile (below 640px) with 16px lateral screen margins. Transitions into an 8-column layout with 24px margins on tablet breakpoints (640px to 1024px).
- **Rhythm & Stacking:** Card containers sit within an 8px vertical rhythm. Related content clusters (guest avatar, stay duration, check-in time) use `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px), while independent cards and insight panels are separated by `card-gap` (12px).
- **Safe Areas & Reachability:** Mobile bottom sheets and floating action modules maintain 24px minimum padding clearance from the hardware home indicator to prevent accidental guest checkout confirmations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through cool, navy-tinted ambient shadows and translucent frosted layers that evoke coastal light, avoiding muddy gray tones.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Background surface rendered in `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card Tier 1 (Level 1):** Resting interactive cards (listings, booking schedules). Styled with a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`), a crisp low-contrast boundary border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`), and an ambient sea-tinted shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.02);`
- **Elevated Interactive Tier (Level 2):** Dragged calendar dates, active reservation popovers, and quick-filter pills:
  `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -4px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.08), 0 3px 6px -2px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.04);`
- **Modal & Bottom Sheet Tier (Level 3):** Bulk pricing updates, dynamic rate overrides, and guest messaging sheets:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 35px -8px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.16), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06);`
- **Coastal Glass Surface:** Bottom tab navigation and sticky rate headers use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88); border-top: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8);`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2**, balancing sleek tech precision with inviting, friendly ergonomics suitable for hospitality software.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Input Fields & Small Buttons:** 8px (`rounded-md`).
- **Standard Cards, Dashboard Panels & Modals:** 16px (`rounded-lg`).
- **Floating Modals & Bottom Sheet Trays:** 24px on top corners (`rounded-t-2xl`).
- **Tags, Season Badges & Filter Pills:** Fully rounded pill-shaped (`9999px`).
- **Listing Media Previews:** 12px (`rounded-md`) to frame property photography neatly within dashboard cards without clipping architectural details.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Ocean Navy (`#0A2540`) with crisp white text, 48px height for touch ergonomics, radius 8px, font weight 600. Subtle oceanic hover/press state shifts to Cerulean (`#0077B6`).
- **Secondary Action:** White background with 1px border in `#E2E8F0` and navy text. Press state applies `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary / Quick Action:** Subtle cyan tint (`rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.12)`) with `#0077B6` label for instant rate adjustments or bulk calendar opens.

### Season & Status Badges (Alta/Baixa Temporada)
- **Alta Temporada (Peak Season):** Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`. Accompanied by a subtle solar icon.
- **Baixa Temporada (Low Season):** Background `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.1)`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
- **Media Temporada (Shoulder):** Background `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.12)`, text `#0077B6`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.25)`.
- **Format:** Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `label-sm`, uppercase tracking, inline flex alignment.

### Cards & Metric Displays
- **Listing Management Card:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding 16px. Top section displays listing thumbnail, neighborhood tag (e.g., *Jurerê Internacional*, *Campeche*, *Lagoa da Conceição*), and current ADR (Average Daily Rate).
- **Yield Trend Card:** Features an emerald chip (`+18.4% vs. last season`), small Sparkline canvas, and single-touch toggle between Gross Revenue and Net Host Payout.

### Interactive Yield & Occupancy Charts
- **Palette in Data Viz:** Area gradients transition from `#00B4D8` (30% opacity) down to zero at base, with stroke in `#0077B6` (2.5px width).
- **High Season Forecast Shading:** Highlighted with an amber vertical band background (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.08)`) with dotted bounds to visually predict occupancy surges during holiday weeks (Reveillon, Carnaval).
- **Scrubber Tooltip:** Deep Navy card tooltip (`#0A2540`) displaying formatted BRL currency in pure white bold type.

### Form Inputs & Date Range Selectors
- **Inputs:** 44px min-height, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, surface `#FFFFFF`. Focus ring: 2px solid `#0077B6` with a faint `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.2)` halo.
- **Multi-Calendar Stepper:** Dual-date picker optimized for mobile check-in/check-out span selections with connected lagoon blue ribbon highlighting reserved nights.
